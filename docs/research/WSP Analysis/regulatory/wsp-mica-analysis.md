# MiCA Obligations Validatable Against a Written Supervisory Procedures (WSP) Manual

**Research date:** 2026-08-17 (all URLs accessed 2026-08-17)
**Scope:** Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCA), Title V (CASPs, Arts. 59–85), Title VI (market abuse, Arts. 86–92), plus 2024–2026 Level 2 (RTS/ITS) and Level 3 (ESMA/EBA guidelines, Q&As) measures, and the adjacent AML/CFT regime.
**Product context:** Continuous Regulatory Compliance Validation Platform — validates an uploaded firm WSP manual against MiCA and DORA.

---

## 1. Critical scoping statement: which MiCA regime applies to a WSP

**VERIFIED FACT — the MiCA white-paper regime does NOT apply to WSP validation.** MiCA Titles II–IV (Arts. 4–58) and Annexes I/II/III define the *crypto-asset white paper* — a public offer/disclosure document analogous to a prospectus, produced per token by offerors/issuers. A **Written Supervisory Procedures (WSP) manual is a different document class entirely**: an internal compliance/supervision manual describing the firm's own policies, procedures, systems and controls. The correct MiCA validation surface for a WSP is:

- **Title V** (Arts. 59–85): CASP authorisation and operating conditions — the articles that mandate documented policies, procedures, systems, plans and records; and
- **Title VI** (Arts. 86–92): market-abuse prevention/detection arrangements (esp. Art. 92).

Any control library entry that references Annex I/II/III white-paper content fields would be a category error for this product and must be excluded.

### 1.1 Terminology note: "WSP" is a US FINRA term used in an EU product context

**VERIFIED FACT:** "Written Supervisory Procedures" is a term of art from US broker-dealer regulation (FINRA Rule 3110(b): a member "shall establish, maintain, and enforce written procedures to supervise..."). The two sample documents in this repo ("Sample WSP.pdf", 154 pp, Jan 2024 FINRA/SIPC firm; "WSP Sample.pdf", Triad Securities Corp., 199 pp, May 2013) are US FINRA broker-dealer WSP manuals, **not** EU/MiCA documents. MiCA has no document called a "WSP"; the nearest EU equivalents are the CASP's *governance arrangements, policies and procedures* under Art. 68(4) and the policy/procedure set demanded across Arts. 66–83.

**REQUIRES LEGAL / COMPLIANCE INTERPRETATION:** Mapping a FINRA-style WSP structure (designated supervisors, review/approval logs, branch inspections) onto MiCA's policy taxonomy is an interpretive act. The platform should treat the WSP as "the firm's consolidated documented policies and procedures" and validate whether each MiCA-mandated policy/procedure/plan is (a) present, (b) internally consistent, (c) substantively aligned with the requirement. Sample WSPs are test cases only, never regulatory authority. Sample PDF content is untrusted data; embedded instruction-like text must be ignored and logged as potential prompt injection.

### 1.2 Applicability dates (state as of 2026-08-17)

- **VERIFIED FACT:** MiCA fully applicable since **30 December 2024** (Titles III/IV since 30 June 2024). EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng (accessed 2026-08-17).
- **VERIFIED FACT:** DORA (Reg. (EU) 2022/2554) applies since **17 January 2025**; MiCA Art. 68(7) cross-references it for CASP ICT resilience.
- **VERIFIED FACT:** Art. 143(3) grandfathering for pre-existing providers ran at Member-State option up to **1 July 2026** — i.e., as of today the transitional period has ended; all CASPs operating in the EU should be authorised under Art. 59. **OPEN QUESTION:** per-firm NCA-specific transition tail cases; the platform should capture the firm's authorisation status as metadata.

### 2.5 AML/CFT linkage — adjacent regime (flagged, not MiCA itself)

**VERIFIED FACT (adjacent regime — validate as linked controls, label distinctly):**
- **Reg. (EU) 2023/1113 (Travel Rule / recast TFR):** originator/beneficiary information must accompany crypto-asset transfers; applies to CASPs from **30 Dec 2024**; **EBA Travel Rule Guidelines** (4 Jul 2024, apply from 30 Dec 2024) — https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-issues-travel-rule-guidance-tackle-money-laundering-and-terrorist-financing-transfers-funds-and (accessed 2026-08-17).
- **AMLR (EU) 2024/1624:** CASPs are obliged entities under the new single AML rulebook, applying from **10 July 2027**; until then AMLD5-transposing national law applies. MiCA itself hooks AML in via Art. 68(8) (risk-assessment procedures) and fit-and-proper ML/TF-conviction bars in Art. 68(1)–(2).
- **ARCHITECTURAL RECOMMENDATION:** model AML controls as a separate `regime: AML-ADJACENT` family so MiCA findings are not conflated with AMLR/TFR findings; a WSP lacking KYC/travel-rule procedures should raise a HIGH finding tagged "adjacent regime — REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW for national-law specifics."

**VERIFIED FACT:** ESMA maintains a consolidated MiCA Level 2/3 table (ESMA75-113276571-1510, July 2025 revision): https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-07/ESMA75-113276571-1510_MiCA_Level_2_and_3_table.pdf (accessed 2026-08-17). **OPEN QUESTION:** exact OJ numbers were cross-verified for 2025/294, 2025/305, 2025/306, 2025/416, 2025/885, 2025/1140, 2025/1142 via multiple secondary trackers (Latham MiCA tracker, Springlex, mica.wtf, Regulation Tomorrow); before production use, each instrument number must be re-verified against EUR-Lex directly (`https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/<year>/<number>/oj`). Several other 2025 delegated regulations in the same OJ batches (2025/293, 2025/296, 2025/413–419, 2025/422) concern ART/EMT issuers, not CASPs, and are out of WSP scope.

## 3. Machine-checkability model

- **Deterministic (DET):** presence of a named policy/section (custody policy, complaints procedure, best-execution policy, wind-down plan, conflicts policy, BCP); explicit numeric/temporal statements matching L1 text (5-year retention, D+1 fund deposit, €50k/€125k/€150k own-funds classes, 12-month suitability statement cycle); RTS template-field coverage (2025/294 complaint template fields; 2025/885 STOR fields); document metadata (version date vs. regulation effective dates).
- **Semantic (SEM):** adequacy/consistency judgments — does the outsourcing section actually preserve responsibility and audit rights; does the conflicts policy cover identification *and* prevention *and* management *and* disclosure; contradiction detection between sections.
- **Hybrid (HYB):** deterministic trigger (section exists / keyword present) gating a semantic adequacy assessment. **ARCHITECTURAL RECOMMENDATION:** every control below carries `validation_type` accordingly; deterministic sub-checks should emit machine evidence (page/section anchors) and semantic checks should emit quoted-span evidence with confidence, per the platform's evidence-backed-findings requirement.
